3. Device Permissions & Hardware Access

We strictly adhere to the principle of least-privilege. EAS-Invoice & Quote Generator requests zero dangerous permissions at runtime:

  • No Location Access: The App does not request or access GPS, coarse location, or network cell telemetry.
  • No Contacts Permission: The App does not read your device contacts address book. All clients are entered manually or imported via explicit user action.
  • No Camera or Microphone Access: The App does not require access to your device camera or audio hardware.
  • Zero-Permission Media Picker: When you choose a company logo or import a backup file, the app uses Android's modern system Photo Picker (PickVisualMedia) and Storage Access Framework (SAF). The app never requests broad storage access (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE).
  • Secure File Sharing: When you share an invoice PDF or CSV export via WhatsApp, Gmail, or Google Drive, the App utilizes Android's secure FileProvider. Temporary read access is strictly scoped to the single file you chose to share, and only granted to the target app you selected.

4. Third-Party Services and Analytics

  • Zero Advertising Networks: The Application contains no banner ads, interstitial ads, video ads, or advertising SDKs.
  • Zero Analytics / Behavioral Trackers: We do not integrate Google Analytics for Firebase, Mixpanel, Facebook SDK, AppsFlyer, or any other tracking tools.
  • Zero Remote API Synchronization: The application does not sync with an external proprietary cloud database. All data persistence is performed locally.

5. Data Security and Cryptography

The security of your business and client records is paramount:

  • Sandbox Isolation: All databases, application preferences, and temporary files are isolated within the Android application sandbox, preventing unauthorized inspection by other applications on your device.
  • Optional AES Encrypted Backups: The App provides built-in military-grade AES (Advanced Encryption Standard) encryption for exported JSON backup archives. You can set a custom master password when creating a backup, preventing unauthorized decryption if the file is copied or shared.
  • User Responsibility: Because all data is stored locally on your device, you are responsible for maintaining the physical security of your smartphone/tablet, including the use of device lock screens (PIN, password, fingerprint, or facial recognition).

6. Data Retention, Portability, and Right to Erasure (Deletion)

Under international privacy laws (GDPR Art. 17, POPIA Sec. 24, CCPA):

  • Data Retention: Records remain stored locally on your device for as long as the application remains installed, or until you choose to delete them.
  • User Control & Deletion: You have immediate and unrestricted access to delete any client, product, document, or snapshot from within the app interface.
  • Complete Data Erasure: You can completely and irreversibly erase all application data at any time by going to your Android device settings: Settings > Apps > EAS-Invoice > Storage > Clear Data, or by uninstalling the application.
  • Data Portability: You can export complete, unencumbered copies of your data at any time in standard JSON or CSV spreadsheet formats.
